# Grade 1 pilot summary

500 text-only questions cover all 198 skills. 104 skills have 3 questions and 94 have 2.

The first 104 phonics skills in catalog order have 3 questions. The last phonics skill, `fam_which_word_does_not_rhyme`, and all 93 grammar, vocabulary, and reading skills have 2. 104 × 3 + 94 × 2 = 500.

No skill was skipped.

## Sources

- CMUdict citation forms: 126052 headwords. A phonics fact is kept only when the citation phonemes match the claim. A different stress-1 vowel drops the word. A stress-0 reduction does not.
- Fry first 300: 300 words.
- Dolch pre-primer 40, primer 52, grade 1 41. Wikipedia grade 1 uses "going". The saved ReadWriteThink PDF was not parsed.
- Dolch nouns used as common content words: 96.
- Other words are common CVC and decodable words whose sounds were confirmed in CMUdict, plus the sight words named in each skill title.
- The old question bank in grade1/pack was not reused.

## Text versions

41 skills are picture_to_word, word_to_picture, listen_tap, or listen-presented. Their 119 questions are marked `[text version]` and test the same skill in words.
